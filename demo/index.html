01 / SYSTEM & ELECTRONICS

The path from skin contact
to a phone display.

The original design couples sensing and reference electrodes to a BMD101 ECG acquisition chip. A TPS79333 supply stage powers the circuit, and a BLE module carries the measurements to the mini program.

02 / ELECTRODES & SOLIDWORKS

Designing the wearable form.

The team explored a petal-shaped sensing region, a circular reference region and a housing for the electronics. SolidWorks modelling is described in the project materials. Exported renders and physical iterations are shown below.

THE MATERIALS CONCEPT

A flexible interface.
A reusable electronics core.

The original design notes propose PU film for the electrode interface, PDMS for flexible moulded parts and PI film for the circuit substrate.

The team also considered replacing the contact elements while retaining the circuit board. These are documented design intentions; final material properties and wear performance were not independently measured in this reconstruction.

04 / SOFTWARE & SIGNALS

Making the measurements visible.

Jinxiao Wan's main documented contribution was WeChat mini-program development. The original source includes device discovery, BLE reception, waveform plotting and spreadsheet export. Signal-processing work was a separate team component.

THE 2026 RECONSTRUCTION

A reproducible software path.

The rebuilt mini program adds a buffered packet decoder, checksum validation, bounded recording, CSV export and actual local history. A separate simulation mode lets the acquisition flow run without a sensor.

The Python analysis reads each lead independently and evaluates R peaks against supplied annotations. The MATLAB baseline and original wavelet approach are retained alongside the first-party source.
