# SolidWorks and mechanical design

The team presentation describes SolidWorks modelling for the sensor shape and electrode design. Its exported renders and prototype photographs are preserved below. Native SolidWorks files were not included.

## Round housing: model and prototype

| --- | --- |
| ![Round housing render](../demo/assets/round-enclosure-render.png) | ![Printed round housing](../demo/assets/round-enclosure-print.jpg) |

The round design includes a recessed feature. The original design text describes a groove for retaining the circuit board. Exact dimensions, tolerances and board-fit measurements are unavailable.

## Petal-and-ring design

![Petal-and-ring electrode arm render](../demo/assets/electrode-arm-render.png)

The elongated concept combines a petal-shaped region and a circular end. The original deck discusses an integrated sensor design and a separated electrode design, as well as reusing the circuit board while replacing contact elements. These are documented design intentions.

![Elongated cover render](../demo/assets/enclosure-cover-render.png)

A second exported render shows an elongated cover with a circular end. The archive does not provide a revision history or an assembly tree linking the two render variants.

## Wearable prototype

![Physical wearable enclosure prototype](../demo/assets/wearable-prototype.jpg)

The original photograph shows a white enclosure prototype with branching arms and a circular centre. It documents a physical iteration of the wearable form. It does not establish material certification or long-term wear performance.
